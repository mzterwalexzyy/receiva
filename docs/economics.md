# Economics

For a 1,000 USDG invoice with a 980 advance and a 100 bond, the funder pays 980 and may receive 1,000. Potential profit is 20, a 2% face-value discount and approximately 2.04% return on the advanced principal for the full term. This is not an annual rate or a guaranteed return.

On-time settlement requires 1,000 USDG from the buyer and returns the 100 bond. Default sends the 100 bond to the funder, leaving 900 outstanding. A later cure transfers 900 to the funder. A direct late settlement has the same economic result and records a default and late repayment.

Bond coverage is 10% of face value. If the buyer never cures, the funder loses 880 of its 980 advance in this example. No blockchain rule can force payment from an unfunded or unwilling buyer.

The hackathon contract takes no fee. Future origination fees and accounting integrations require legal, compliance and commercial validation before launch.
